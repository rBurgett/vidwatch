Appearance

Kids mode

When kids mode is on, anything above the limit is hidden everywhere, the “More videos” list and autoplay are turned off, and the search box is hidden — so kids only see the curated home grid.

Recommendations

There’s never autoplay into recommended videos — auto-advance only happens inside a playlist or your play queue.
